KoreanMemo Sans Regular is a renamed, subsetted derivative of
Noto Sans CJK SC Regular version 2.004.

Original font copyright: © 2014-2021 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/).
Upstream project: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
Release Sans2.004, commit 523d033d6cb47f4a80c58a35753646f5c3608a78.

This font and its modifications are licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1; see the accompanying OFL.txt. The original copyright and
license metadata are also retained inside the font.

The subset contains all 11,172 modern precomposed Hangul syllables,
Hangul Jamo supported by the original font, GB2312 characters, Chinese
characters used in the bundled vocabulary and UI, Latin and punctuation.
It is not restricted to Hangul characters found in the bundled words.
The family is renamed KoreanMemo Sans. Vertical and regional alternate
features are omitted; horizontal Korean shaping features are retained.
